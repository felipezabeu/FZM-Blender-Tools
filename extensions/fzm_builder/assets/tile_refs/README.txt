FZM directional porcelain textures — finishes 05, 06 and 07

The packaged WebP textures are generated approximations of the corresponding
FZM catalogue tile appearances, not the individual product photographs and
not a guarantee of any supplier's exact pattern, palette or dimensions.
The material selects faces deterministically per tile, without mirroring or
rotating them. No network access is needed to render or edit the material.
